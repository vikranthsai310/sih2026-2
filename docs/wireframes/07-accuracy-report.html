07 · Accuracy Report (S9)

09 §5.6 · Personas: analyst, commander · GET /api/v1/runs/{id}/report?format=json|html|pdf, /files/runs/{id}/09_qa/accuracy.json
  1. Downloads in PDF, self-contained HTML and JSON. The PDF has a bilingual title block.
  2. Method statement first: number of checkpoints, their survey accuracy, independence. Under 30 → ⚠ "indicative only, not ASPRS Ed.2 compliant". No checkpoints → see states.
  3. KPI tiles: the number stays in text colour; status is the icon + word beneath. Absolute and relative accuracy are separate tiles (charter principle 2).
  4. Before/after table: the core proof. Formulas in the caption. PASS is never shown without checkpoints.
  5. Bias vs noise on a shared 0–4 m axis so the reduction is seen at true size. Bar = RMSE; bias solid, noise hatched (share of RMSE²). Hover a bar for exact values.
  6. Residual map: vectors with their exaggeration factor and a scale; checkpoints link to table rows.
  7. Doming: line with its ± band against dashed, labelled target lines (not coloured as pass/fail).
  8. Relative length test reported separately from absolute placement.
